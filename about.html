About the concept

From Jerez traditions to Valley tables.

The real restaurant says the Huerta family has shared cherished recipes and flavors inspired by Jerez, Zacatecas, with Los Angeles since 1998. This prototype imagines that story as a celebration of the communal cooking traditions of central Mexico: slow-cooked meats, chiles, salsas, beans, tortillas and generous platters meant to bring people together.

The imagined design story also draws on the restaurant’s visible pride in Mexican heritage, pairing food with colorful artwork and references to the history and culture of Zacatecas.

Fictionalized for this prototype: The culinary narrative in the preceding paragraph is creative copy, not a verified biography of the owners.
